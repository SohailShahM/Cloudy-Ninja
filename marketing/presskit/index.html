About

Cloudy Ninja is a momentum-based 2D pixel-art platformer about restoring corrupted ecosystems. Players switch between three characters — each with a distinct ability tied to the water cycle — across hand-crafted levels that double as light interactive science lessons. It targets casual-to-enthusiast platformer fans who want satisfying movement and a reason to care about what they're doing.

Switch between Ebo, Laya, and Zephyr mid-jump to reach what one of them couldn't. Eight stages culminate in a multi-phase Storm Sentinel boss arena. The in-game Cloud Atlas pulls from real NOAA and NASA sources, so every entry the player collects is a piece of grounded climate education — not flavor text.

Key features

  • Three-character switching — Ebo's Seed Slam, Laya's Wind Dash, Zephyr's Float; swap mid-air to chain abilities across one move.
  • 8 levels + Storm Sentinel boss — Sky Sanctuary hub, four tutorial stages, three campaign stages, and a three-phase boss fight.
  • 12 achievements — All unlockable in Assist Mode; no gates on accessibility settings.
  • Accessibility-first design — Color-blind safe palette, reduced motion, full key/button rebinding, gamepad-ready, 4K / HiDPI scaling.
  • Assist Mode — Toggleable invincibility, infinite spirit charges, and a slow-speed slider for motor accessibility.
  • Cloud Atlas — In-game collectible entries sourced from real NOAA / NASA climate research.
  • 3 save slots + checkpoint autosave — Atomic writes; runs are never half-saved.
  • Cross-platform — Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux) and Android, with mobile two-thumb controls.
