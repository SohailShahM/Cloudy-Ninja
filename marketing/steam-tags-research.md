# Steam Tags + Keyword Research for Cloudy Ninja

Research-only deliverable for ticket **T-075**. Compiled 2026-05-12. Numbers (review counts, percentages) are snapshots from the dates listed in source links and will drift; positioning conclusions are stable.

The goal: lock in the right tag combination for Cloudy Ninja's eventual Steam listing — concrete enough that when we publish the store page, we don't have to relitigate every choice. Steam allows up to **20 user-applied tags + 5 publisher tags** per game; the **top 5** carry the most algorithmic weight (Tag Wizard prioritizes high-information sub-genre tags; "Indie" and "Singleplayer" are explicitly de-weighted as low-information). Tag overlap with "similar" games is what drives the "More Like This" recommendations and the personalized queue. See **section (f)** for sources behind these claims.

Cloudy Ninja in one line: a 2D pixel-art platformer about restoring corrupted ecosystems, with three switchable characters whose water-cycle abilities solve traversal puzzles, an accessibility-first design (Assist Mode, color-blind palettes, reduced-motion, key rebinding), and a Storm Sentinel boss. Target price $2.99–4.99, launching itch.io first, Steam later.

Observations across the sample:

- **Every genre-cluster game uses `Pixel Graphics` or `Hand-drawn` + `2D` + `Platformer`** in its top 10. These three are the spine.
- **`Metroidvania` outperforms `Precision Platformer` in raw discovery** (Hollow Knight + Dead Cells + Ori + Animal Well + Chicory all carry it; only Celeste anchors purely on "Precision Platformer"). Whether that tag fits Cloudy Ninja honestly is question (e) below.
- **`Difficult` is shared by Celeste, Hollow Knight, Dead Cells, Ori.** It's a high-information tag for the platformer audience. Cloudy Ninja's Assist Mode means we *can't* claim it without diluting the meaning. This is an explicit anti-tag for us — see section (e).
- **The eco cluster never uses `Education` or `Educational`** despite being conservation-themed (Terra Nil, Endling, Alba). It's a deliberate avoidance — that tag is a discoverability trap in the consumer-game market. See section (e).
- **`Nature` is doing real work** for Terra Nil, Endling, Alba, A Short Hike. It's the cleanest tag for our eco angle that doesn't reek of edutainment.
- **`Cute` + `Colorful` + `Atmospheric`** is the wholesome-cluster spine. Eight of twelve games carry at least two; six carry all three. Audience-broadening without diluting positioning.
- **`Family Friendly`** appears on Terra Nil, A Short Hike, Alba, Chicory — never on the difficulty-forward platformers. Compatible with our Assist Mode story; we should include it.
- **`Female Protagonist`** appears on Celeste, Endling, Alba, A Short Hike. Cloudy Ninja has three switchable characters (Ebo, Laya, Zephyr); we should confirm which qualify before claiming this. Treat as stretch, pending T-046b/character-design confirmation.

## (b) How Steam's algorithm weights tags (mechanics that drove these picks)

Three documented mechanics, all of which point in the same direction: **pick fewer, more specific tags rather than more, broader ones.**

1. **Top-5 weighting.** Steam's Tag Wizard now visibly re-ranks tags by information content. "Indie" and "Singleplayer" auto-sink to the bottom of the prioritized list. The top 5 tags get extra weight in Featured & Recommended and the "More Like This" carousel. So we should not waste top-5 slots on generic descriptors. ([Steamworks tags doc](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/tags); [Simon Carless / GameDiscover](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/are-you-prioritizing-your-steam-tags))
2. **20-tag similarity overlap.** The top 20 tags determine what counts as "similar" to your game for the recommender. Overlap matters more than order: more shared tags with a hit = more chance Steam shows our game to people who liked the hit. ([Steam Data Suite tag guide](https://steamdatasuite.com/choosing-the-right-tags-to-drive-discoverability/); [ECI Games 2025 guide](https://www.ecigames.net/media/eci-games-2025-steam-discoverability-guide))
3. **Irrelevant tags actively hurt.** Tagging a game inaccurately puts it in front of people who immediately click away; Steam reads "low click-through from this audience" as a quality signal against your page and de-prioritizes it everywhere. The October 2025 algorithm update over-corrected away from tag-similarity, was rolled back by the end of the month, but the principle stayed: only tag what your game actually is. ([Game Developer / Carless](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/the-state-of-the-algorithm-what-s-happening-to-indies-on-steam-); [Phiture optimization guide](https://phiture.com/asostack/game-steam-page-optimization/))

The practical translation for Cloudy Ninja: 8–12 tags, very specific, no aspirational descriptors, no contradictions.

## (c) Recommended must-have primary tag set (4 tags)

These four go into the top-5 slots. They are the spine — every comparable game in cluster 1 carries the first three; the fourth is the angle that distinguishes us from a generic precision-platformer pitch.

### 1. `Pixel Graphics`
The single most-shared tag across our genre cluster — Celeste, Dead Cells, Pizza Tower, Animal Well, A Short Hike all carry it. It is the visual-style tag that platformer fans actually filter on, and Steam's recommender treats it as a primary sub-genre signal because pixel-art audiences self-select hard. Non-negotiable for Cloudy Ninja (Kenney `pixel-platformer` base pack, 32×32 base resolution, T-046 pixel-art overhaul scoped).

**3 comparable games:** Celeste (97%, 140k reviews) · Pizza Tower (98%, 44k reviews) · Dead Cells (96%, 43k reviews) · A Short Hike (99%, 12k reviews — uses it despite being 3D).

### 2. `Platformer`
The genre tag. Combined with `Pixel Graphics` and `2D` it forms a tight recommender cluster. We don't claim `Precision Platformer` because Assist Mode makes that dishonest; we don't claim `Action Platformer` because momentum + traversal puzzles are the loop, not enemies. Just `Platformer` — broad enough to encompass our switchable-character traversal-puzzle hybrid, specific enough to be useful.

**3 comparable games:** Celeste · Hollow Knight (541k reviews, 97%) · Ori and the Blind Forest (30k, 94%).

### 3. `2D`
Reinforces the genre cluster. Appears in the top 10 of every comparable game in cluster 1 except A Short Hike (which is 3D). Steam treats `2D` + `Platformer` + `Pixel Graphics` as the canonical sub-genre signature; the recommender weights this combination heavily for the "people who liked Celeste" cohort.

**3 comparable games:** Hollow Knight · Dead Cells · Chicory: A Colorful Tale (3.3k, 97%).

### 4. `Nature`
This is our angle. It's the cleanest non-edutainment way to signal the eco theme. Terra Nil, Endling, and Alba all use it as a core discoverability tag; A Short Hike carries it too. It connects us to a real, well-defined Steam audience — players who buy nature/conservation-themed games — without forcing us to claim educational positioning that hurts conversion in the gaming market (see section e).

**3 comparable games:** Terra Nil (86%, 4.7k reviews) · Endling — Extinction is Forever (94%, 1.5k reviews) · Alba: A Wildlife Adventure (97%, 3.1k reviews) · A Short Hike (99%, 12k reviews).

> **Why not 5 must-haves?** We deliberately leave the 5th top-slot for the strongest stretch tag (likely `Cute` or `Atmospheric` — see below). This keeps optionality during the actual store-page upload, when we can A/B briefly between two candidates without risking the spine.

## (d) Recommended stretch tag set (7 tags)

These broaden the wishlist without diluting the spine. Each one is a tag where Cloudy Ninja can honestly claim membership, where at least 3 surveyed comparables succeed with it, and where the audience-expansion is bigger than the risk of mismatch.

### 5. `Cute`
The single highest-leverage stretch tag in our sample — six of twelve comparables carry it, including the difficult genre giants (Celeste, Hollow Knight) AND the wholesome cluster (A Short Hike, Alba, Chicory, Animal Well). Combines well with `Difficult`-cluster discovery without locking us into edginess. Our switchable-character cast + Kenney pixel-platformer art reads cute already.

**3 comparable games:** Celeste · Animal Well · Alba: A Wildlife Adventure · Chicory: A Colorful Tale.

### 6. `Atmospheric`
Heaviest-used mood tag across both genre and eco clusters. Hollow Knight, Ori, Animal Well, Terra Nil, Endling, Beacon Pines, Alba — seven of twelve. Cloudy Ninja's three ambient music tracks + ScreenFade + parallax background system are doing exactly the work this tag describes. Free signal.

**3 comparable games:** Hollow Knight · Ori and the Blind Forest · Endling — Extinction is Forever · Animal Well.

### 7. `Exploration`
Shared by both eco-cluster (Alba, A Short Hike, Endling, Terra Nil-adjacent) and genre-cluster (Hollow Knight, Animal Well, Celeste). Our Cloud Atlas pickup loop is literally exploration-reward gameplay. Steam treats this tag as a strong wholesome-audience signal — combines well with `Nature` to define our niche precisely.

**3 comparable games:** Hollow Knight · A Short Hike · Animal Well · Alba: A Wildlife Adventure.

### 8. `Colorful`
Eco cluster + wholesome cluster shared. Terra Nil, Endling, Alba, A Short Hike, Chicory, Beacon Pines all carry it. Differentiates us from grimdark indie platformers (Hollow Knight, Dead Cells) without claiming "easy." Cloudy Ninja's three biomes (sky/eco/arid) read colorful by design.

**3 comparable games:** Terra Nil · Chicory: A Colorful Tale · Beacon Pines · Alba: A Wildlife Adventure.

### 9. `Side Scroller`
Disambiguates "platformer" — we are *not* a top-down platformer or 3D platformer. Celeste, Endling, and Pizza Tower all use it. Tightens the recommender cluster. Cost: minimal.

**3 comparable games:** Celeste · Pizza Tower · Endling — Extinction is Forever · Hollow Knight.

### 10. `Family Friendly`
Honest fit given our Assist Mode + accessibility-first design + non-violent core loop (Seed Slam defeats are bonk-not-gore). Carried by Terra Nil, Alba, Chicory, A Short Hike — the entire wholesome cluster. Opens us to the parents-buying-for-kids segment which is huge for $2.99–4.99 indies. Risk: pairs poorly with `Difficult`, but we are *not* claiming `Difficult`, so the conflict doesn't fire.

**3 comparable games:** Terra Nil · Alba: A Wildlife Adventure · A Short Hike · Chicory: A Colorful Tale.

### 11. `Great Soundtrack`
A user-applied tag in Steam's taxonomy that audiences only actually use when the music is genuinely memorable. Celeste, Hollow Knight, Ori, Chicory, A Short Hike all earn it. Cloudy Ninja's three ambient tracks + 1.5s crossfade are deliberate, and `ProceduralMusicGenerator` produces water-cycle-themed cues — listing this tag claims that aspiration, but the tag only sticks if reviewers reinforce it. **Caveat:** if reviewers don't independently apply this within the first ~50 reviews, drop it from publisher tags and let it die naturally. Don't fight users on a music tag.

**3 comparable games:** Celeste · Hollow Knight · Ori and the Blind Forest · Chicory: A Colorful Tale.

## (e) Anti-tags — combinations and tags to actively avoid

Six tags that would hurt Cloudy Ninja's discovery despite looking attractive. The principle: every tag is a contract with the audience that clicks through. Misrepresentations cost us conversion + Steam algorithm trust.

### Anti-tag 1: `Difficult`
**Why it's tempting:** Celeste, Hollow Knight, Dead Cells, Ori — the four most-comparable platformers all carry it. Skipping this tag feels like leaving the genre cluster.

**Why we can't claim it:** Assist Mode (invincibility, infinite spirits, slow-speed slider) is shipped. Color-blind palettes, reduced-motion mode, full key rebinding are shipped. Steam audiences who filter for `Difficult` expect Celeste B-Side punishment. We will get refunds + negative reviews if we sell to that cohort. The accessibility-first design is a real positioning choice and `Difficult` would actively undermine it.

**Mitigation:** Lean into `Family Friendly` + `Casual`-adjacent stretch tags instead. Accept that we'll lose some Celeste-cohort discovery — we gain a much larger Alba / A Short Hike cohort in exchange.

### Anti-tag 2: `Precision Platformer`
Same problem as `Difficult`, narrower. The Celeste audience uses this tag to find Super Meat Boy successors. Cloudy Ninja's momentum-based movement is Celeste-calibrated, but the level design is traversal-puzzle-and-eco-narrative, not pixel-perfect-spike-rooms. Claiming this tag invites direct comparison we will lose.

### Anti-tag 3: `Metroidvania`
**Why it's tempting:** Hollow Knight, Dead Cells, Animal Well, Ori all use it, and our switchable-character ability gating *kind of* maps onto traversal abilities.

**Why we shouldn't:** Metroidvania carries an explicit promise — interconnected map, backtracking with new abilities to unlock previously-blocked paths, gradual world-revealing. Cloudy Ninja's 7 levels + boss are linear with hub (Sky Sanctuary). We'd lose this audience immediately on the first level-select screen. **Conflict:** `Metroidvania` + linear level structure is the kind of tag mismatch that drops the page in the recommender. Skip it.

### Anti-tag 4: `Educational` / `Education`
**Why it's tempting:** The eco angle, Cloud Atlas climate-source bundling, water-cycle-themed abilities — it's textbook (literally) educational content.

**Why it's a trap:** Zero of our eco cluster comparables use it. Terra Nil, Endling, Alba all deliberately tag as `Nature` + `Atmospheric` instead. The Steam `Education` tag bucket is dominated by school-licensing software and edutainment titles that the consumer-gaming audience filters *out*. Conversion rate from `Educational` browse traffic to wishlists is notoriously poor on Steam. The educational angle is **marketing copy**, not a tag.

### Anti-tag 5: `Casual` + `Difficult` simultaneously
The classic dilution combo. We are not at risk of this because we already chose not to claim `Difficult`. But there's a related trap: claiming `Casual` AND `Platformer` AND `Family Friendly` AND `Cute` would over-bias us into the cozy market. **Recommendation:** include `Family Friendly` and `Cute`, do NOT include `Casual` as a top-20 tag. Cloudy Ninja's core loop is platforming + traversal puzzles; `Casual` would lie about that.

### Anti-tag 6: `Walking Simulator`
Appears on Endling, A Short Hike, Alba — even though those games have real mechanics. Including it would pull in players expecting Dear-Esther-grade no-fail exploration. Cloudy Ninja has fail states (death, hazards, the Storm Sentinel boss). Skip.

### Anti-combo: `Indie` as a top-3 tag
Steam's Tag Wizard now actively de-prioritizes `Indie` because it carries almost zero information (>90% of Steam releases tag themselves Indie). We will *include* it in the 20-tag set because it qualifies us for indie-specific curators and sales, but it should land at position 12+ in the popularity-ordered list. Never use a top-5 slot on it. ([Simon Carless](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/are-you-prioritizing-your-steam-tags))

## (g) Implementation notes for whoever uploads the Steam page

1. **Use the Tag Wizard.** Steam's tool shows the inferred information-content rank in real time. Confirm `Indie` auto-drops to slot 11+ before saving.
2. **Don't seed 20.** Start with these 11. Users will add more (we genuinely want `Great Soundtrack`, `Female Protagonist`, etc. to come from organic reviews, not us claiming them).
3. **Watch the first 30 reviews.** If the top user-applied tag drift is toward something we didn't list (e.g. reviewers keep adding `Cozy` or `Wholesome`), incorporate the strongest 1–2 organic tags into our publisher list and drop our weakest. The 10-day data window after launch is the only time to course-correct cheaply.
4. **Cross-check against itch.io tags.** Cloudy Ninja launches on itch.io first (per GAME_PLAN Sprint D). The itch tag system is looser but the wishlist-from-itch-to-Steam pipeline benefits when both pages signal the same audience. Map: itch `pixel-art` → Steam `Pixel Graphics`; itch `platformer` → Steam `Platformer`; itch `nature` → Steam `Nature`. See `marketing/itch-listing-style-guide.md` for itch-side guidance.
