# Audio Candidates

Research for CC0 / CC-BY ambient music tracks and supplementary SFX to replace the three procedurally-generated ambient tracks (`ambient_arid`, `ambient_wind`, `ambient_eco`) and expand the audio palette for menu / boss contexts. All candidates were verified live at research time and confirm a permissive license (CC0 unless otherwise noted; CC-BY entries require attribution). Sources: OpenGameArt.org, Freesound.org, Free Music Archive, Kenney.nl. Lengths and formats are taken from the source page; "—" means the page did not display a duration.

## Recommended picks

- **ARID — top pick:** *Desert Loop* (`opengameart.org/content/desert-loop`) — CC0, pre-looped, oriental VGM mood is a clean drop-in for `ambient_arid` without attribution overhead.
- **WIND — top pick:** *Space Winds* (`opengameart.org/content/space-winds`) — CC0, lonely background drone matches the "sky/storm" mood better than literal wind FX and layers well over gameplay.
- **ECO — top pick:** *Forest Ambience* by TinyWorlds (`opengameart.org/content/forest-ambience`) — CC0, seamless loop, 6.7k downloads of community validation; ideal direct replacement for `ambient_eco`.
- **MENU — top pick:** *Chill Main Menu music* (`opengameart.org/content/chill-main-menu-music`) — CC0/OGA-BY dual, OGG format (libGDX-friendly), explicitly engineered as a loadable + loopable menu bed.
- **BOSS — top pick:** *Boss Battle Music* by Juhani Junkala (`opengameart.org/content/boss-battle-music`) — CC0, seamlessly looping WAV, widely vetted across indie titles; orchestral mood fits "restoring corrupted ecosystem" climax beats.
- **SFX top 3:** **Kenney — Interface Sounds** (100-clip CC0 pack covers every UI need in one drop), **Fantozzi's Footsteps** (mixed terrain pack already split into grass/sand/stone — maps directly to ARID/ECO themes), **8Bit Coin Collection** (CC0 chiptune pickup that pairs with the project's pixel-art aesthetic without licensing strings).
