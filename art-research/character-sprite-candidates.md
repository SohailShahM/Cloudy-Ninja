### Notes
- **Perspective Check:** All characters listed are strictly side-scrolling profile/3-quarter views appropriate for a standard 2D platformer. Top-down RPG sprites were excluded.
- **Kenney Variants:** The Kenney `pixel-platformer` base pack actually includes multiple character color palettes by default. Using the built-in color variants (Green for Ebo, Blue for Laya) is the safest option for a 5/5 art style match.
